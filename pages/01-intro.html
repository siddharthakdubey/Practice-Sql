The one idea: a query is a pipeline of tables

Every SQL step takes a table in and gives a table out. FROM gives you a table, WHERE shrinks it, GROUP BY collapses it, SELECT reshapes it, ORDER BY sorts it. Even a join or a subquery just produces another table. Once you see this, complex queries become small steps in a row.

RememberSQL is declarative: you describe what result you want, not how to loop over rows. The database engine (the optimizer) decides the how. That is why the same result can have very different speed, which is the topic of the advanced chapters.
